\block{1.\ Motivation}{
\textbf{Surface codes already measure syndromes every round}\,---\,bits generated
for decoding, then discarded.

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[draw=Teal, line width=1mm, rounded corners=6pt, fill=Cloud,

   \textit{``Can we read the device's own fault structure out of data we
   \underline{already} collect for QEC?''}};
\end{tikzpicture}

\vspace{2mm}
Long-term vision: a \textbf{closed loop} recycling QEC data into device knowledge,
then back into better decoding:

\begin{center}

\block{2.\ Case Study \& Problem}{
\textbf{Setting.} Distance-3 rotated surface code (17 qubits); each extraction
round issues \textbf{24 directed CNOTs}, one of which is the \emph{dominant} fault
source.

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.62,
  data/.style={circle, draw=NavyDeep, line width=0.7mm, fill=white,
     minimum size=11mm, font=\small\bfseries},
  zanc/.style={rectangle, draw=Ocean, line width=0.6mm, fill=Teal!25,
     minimum size=8mm, font=\scriptsize},
  xanc/.style={diamond, draw=Coral, line width=0.6mm, fill=Amber!30,
     minimum size=9mm, font=\scriptsize}]
  \foreach \r in {0,1,2}{\foreach \c in {0,1,2}{
      \pgfmathtruncatemacro{\id}{\r*3+\c}
      \node[data] (d\id) at (\c*3.0, -\r*3.0) {\id};}}
  % interior weight-4 plaquettes (checkerboard)
  \node[zanc] at (1.5,-1.5) {Z}; \node[xanc] at (4.5,-1.5) {X};
  \node[xanc] at (1.5,-4.5) {X}; \node[zanc] at (4.5,-4.5) {Z};
  % boundary weight-2 stabilizers (diagonally offset, rotated-code style)
  \node[xanc] at (1.5, 1.5)  {X};   % top
  \node[zanc] at (7.5,-1.5)  {Z};   % right
  \node[zanc] at (-1.5,-4.5) {Z};   % left
  \node[xanc] at (4.5,-7.5)  {X};   % bottom
\end{tikzpicture}\\[1mm]
{\small\itshape Rotated $d{=}3$ layout: 9 data qubits (circles), 8 Z-/X-stabilizer
ancillas (squares / diamonds).}
\end{center}

\vspace{2mm}
\innerblock{The question}{
From the syndrome sequence $S=(s_1,\dots,s_T)$ \emph{alone}, identify the
\textbf{dominant faulty CNOT} among the 24 --- with the fault's rounds and Pauli
type ($XX,\dots,ZZ$) unobserved.}
}

%---------------------------------------------------------------
\block{3.\ Single-Shot Ambiguity}{
Is one measurement enough? \textbf{No.} With the Pauli type unknown, distinct
(CNOT, Pauli) faults produce \emph{identical} syndromes:

\vspace{2mm}
\keyfig{figures/fig_collisions.png}{0.44\linewidth}{%
  Collision map: cell $(i,j)$ counts Pauli pairs for which faults at CNOT$_i$ and
  CNOT$_j$ are indistinguishable.}

\vspace{2mm}
\innerblock{}{
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}c@{\hspace{10mm}}c@{}}
{\LARGE\color{Coral}\textbf{216 $\rightarrow$ 123}} &
{\LARGE\color{Coral}\textbf{72}} \\[0.5mm]
{\small unique signatures} & {\small cross-CNOT collision pairs} \\
\end{tabular}

\vspace{2.5mm}
{\Large\bfseries Single-shot 24-class CNOT identification is\\[0.3mm]
{\color{Coral}information-theoretically impossible.}}

\vspace{1mm}
{\small Invariant under round count and reset mode.}}
}

\block{4.\ Temporal Information Breaks the Ambiguity}{
The way out is \textbf{temporal structure}: reading the fault across many rounds.
The same fault is probed either \emph{temporally} (a long syndrome stream) or
\emph{spatially} (a bag of repeated single-round shots):

\vspace{2mm}
\begin{center}
\scalebox{1.72}{%
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=1, cell/.style={draw=Ocean, minimum size=3.2mm,
     inner sep=0pt, line width=0.3mm}, on/.style={fill=NavyDeep},
     off/.style={fill=white}]
  % --- temporal (T x 8) ---
  \begin{scope}[shift={(0,0)}]
    \foreach \r in {0,...,5}{\foreach \c in {0,...,7}{
       \pgfmathparse{(mod(\r*3+\c*2+1,5)<2)?"on":"off"}\edef\st{\pgfmathresult}
       \node[cell,\st] at (\c*0.34,-\r*0.34) {};}}
    \node[font=\footnotesize] at (1.2,0.5) {8 stabilizers};
    \draw[-{Stealth[length=3mm]}, Teal, line width=0.7mm]
       (-0.45,0.1) -- (-0.45,-1.8) node[midway,left,font=\footnotesize,rotate=90,anchor=south]{$T$ rounds};
    \node[font=\footnotesize\itshape, text width=3.4cm, align=center] at (1.2,-2.7)
       {\textbf{Temporal:} ordered $(T\times8)$ stream};
  \end{scope}
  % --- spatial (bag of N shots) ---
  \begin{scope}[shift={(5.2,0)}]
    \foreach \r in {0,...,3}{\foreach \c in {0,...,7}{
       \pgfmathparse{(mod(\r*5+\c*3+2,7)<3)?"on":"off"}\edef\st{\pgfmathresult}
       \node[cell,\st] at (\c*0.34,-\r*0.48) {};}}
    \node[font=\footnotesize] at (1.2,0.5) {8 stabilizers};
    \draw[decorate, decoration={brace, amplitude=4pt}, line width=0.6mm, Amber]
       (-0.35,0.15) -- (-0.35,-1.55) node[midway,left,font=\footnotesize,xshift=-2pt]{$N$};
    \node[font=\footnotesize\itshape, text width=3.6cm, align=center] at (1.2,-2.7)
       {\textbf{Spatial:} unordered bag of $N$ shots};
  \end{scope}
\end{tikzpicture}}
\end{center}

\vspace{8mm}
\keyfig{figures/fig_acc_vs_N.png}{\linewidth}{%
  Accuracy vs bag size $N$. At $N{=}1$ (single shot) accuracy is near random
  ($1/24$); by \bignum{$N{=}300$}{} it reaches $\approx0.9$\,---\,identifiability is
  restored by \textbf{accumulating information across rounds/shots}.}
}

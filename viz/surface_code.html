Rotated surface code — syndrome over rounds (general distance d, constant-depth schedule)

Place one or more single-qubit Pauli errors on data qubits, each at the round of your choice, and watch the syndrome measurement sequence form across rounds. Every syndrome is produced by actually simulating the circuit (multiple errors combine by XOR — the circuit is Clifford). No decoder here, so any number of errors is allowed.

Click a data qubit inside any round panel to place the brush Pauli there (click again to remove). X error flips Z-stabilizers; Z flips X; Y flips both. Round 0 is the projection round (`|0…0⟩ → |0_L⟩`) and is not injectable.

Constant-depth CNOT schedule

Every round is 4 CNOT ticks regardless of d — all ancillas act in parallel. X-stabilizer: ancilla is control (after H). Z-stabilizer: data is control.

3 · Constant-depth CNOT schedule

tick 0/3
Every round is 4 CNOT ticks regardless of d — all ancillas act in parallel. X-stabilizer: ancilla is control (after H). Z-stabilizer: data is control.

4 · Mid-cycle CNOT fault (hook error)

A 2-qubit Pauli injected right after a CNOT can spread to two data qubits within the same round — a “hook” error. Its syndrome differs from any single-qubit data error.
